# Product Context: Bolton Caterers

## Purpose & Target Audience
Bolton Caterers is a hyper-local, community-first web platform connecting Bolton residents (particularly the South Asian & British Muslim community, families, and event planners) with verified halal caterers, tiffin kitchens, and daily meal providers (e.g. Bajibhai Catering, Royal Exotic, Al-Hashmi's Kitchen).

## Core UX Principles & Audience Empathy
1. **Audience Demographics**: Multi-generational users, including older family members and non-native English speakers.
2. **Clarity & Legibility**: High contrast, crisp typography (no tiny illegible gray text), large comfortable tap targets (≥ 44px), explicit actions ("Order on WhatsApp", "Call").
3. **No Fake Gimmicks**: No fake star ratings, no artificial review counters, no generic SaaS purple gradients or template fluff.
4. **WhatsApp Lift-and-Shift**: Authentic kitchen-to-customer connection. Caterers upload their daily paper/WhatsApp flyer; customers browse the daily specials and order directly via WhatsApp or quick phone call.
5. **Mobile-First, Large-Screen Responsive**: Handheld phone experience is primary (feels like a native mobile web app with instant tap-to-expand lightbox), but on tablet and desktop screens, the layout scales gracefully into an elegant multi-column grid with a dedicated sticky navigation/filter header.
